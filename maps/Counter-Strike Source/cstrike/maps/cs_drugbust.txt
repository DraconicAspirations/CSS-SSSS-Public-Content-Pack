A remake of the classic cs_crackhouse map for Counter Strike made by [GaD]Renegade a.k.a Wrenny.

========
Backstory
========

New York City, police forces have been dispatched to clear and take down a small crackhouse within the industial section of the city. Suspects inside have taken hostages and have fired upon police officers, S.W.A.T. has been authorized to use force and breach the building.
